# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Keepstack to be a project anyone can contribute to. We pledge to make participation a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people new to the stack
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully, and giving it the same way
- Focusing on what's best for the project and its users
- Assuming good faith when something reads badly in text

Behaviour that doesn't:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal and political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when someone is
representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through the contact listed in `docs/SECURITY.md`.
Reports are handled privately and in confidence. Maintainers who don't follow this code in good
faith may face consequences determined by other maintainers.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that don't align with this Code of Conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when asked.
